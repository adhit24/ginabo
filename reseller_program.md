# ROLE
You are a senior conversion-focused web designer, UX strategist, and direct response copywriter.

Your task is to create a high-converting landing page for a partnership program.

---

# PROJECT NAME
Ginabo Partner Program (GPP)

---

# OBJECTIVE
Create a landing page that:
- Attracts potential resellers, stockists, and distributors
- Builds trust quickly
- Clearly explains the business opportunity
- Drives users to register / join via WhatsApp or form

---

# TARGET AUDIENCE
- Women (primary)
- Age 18–35
- Students, side hustlers, young entrepreneurs
- People who want extra income or start small business
- Beginners with little to no selling experience

---

# CORE OFFER
Ginabo Partner Program is a business opportunity where members can:
- Buy products at partner price
- Resell for profit
- Get guidance, training, and support
- Access rewards and incentives

---

# KEY MESSAGES
- “Jualan jadi lebih mudah, bukan sendirian”
- “Sudah ada sistem, tinggal jalan”
- “Cocok untuk pemula”
- “Modal ringan, margin jelas”
- “Dibimbing sampai bisa jualan”

---

# TONE & STYLE
- Friendly, persuasive, and empowering
- Not too corporate
- Clear, simple, and benefit-driven
- Use short sentences
- Avoid jargon
- Focus on emotional + logical triggers

---

# PAGE STRUCTURE

## 1. HERO SECTION
Goal: Grab attention & communicate value instantly

Include:
- Headline (strong, benefit-driven)
- Subheadline (clarify offer)
- CTA button (Join Now / Daftar Sekarang)
- Visual direction (active women, healthy lifestyle)

---

## 2. PROBLEM SECTION
Explain pain points:
- Bingung mulai bisnis
- Takut gak laku
- Gak punya sistem
- Jualan sendirian

---

## 3. SOLUTION (INTRO GPP)
Introduce Ginabo Partner Program as the solution:
- Sistem sudah disiapkan
- Produk sudah ada
- Tinggal jualan

---

## 4. BENEFITS SECTION
Break into clear blocks:

- Margin Lebar
- Reward Penjualan
- Bimbingan & Training
- Free Sample

Each benefit must:
- Have a bold title
- Short explanation
- Real impact to user

---

## 5. PROFIT SIMULATION
Show simple example:

Example:
- Buy price vs selling price
- Profit per product
- Monthly potential

Make it easy to understand

---

## 6. REWARD SYSTEM
Explain:
- Bulanan
- Quartal
- Tahunan

Highlight:
- Bonus
- Hadiah
- Recognition

---

## 7. PRODUCT PREVIEW
Show:
- GlowAge Serum
- Moisture Cream
- Bright Renewal Set

Include:
- Price comparison (optional visual table)
- Positioning (affordable skincare)

---

## 8. HOW IT WORKS
Step-by-step:

1. Daftar jadi partner  
2. Dapat harga khusus  
3. Mulai jualan  
4. Dapat profit & reward  

---

## 9. MARKETING SUPPORT
Explain ecosystem:

- Event (Sport & Healthy campaign)
- Campus activation
- Website & landing page
- WhatsApp closing system
- SEO & marketplace

Goal:
Make user feel “tidak mulai dari nol”

---

## 10. SOCIAL PROOF (OPTIONAL BUT RECOMMENDED)
- Testimonials
- Partner success stories
- Before-after income (if available)

---

## 11. CTA SECTION (STRONG CLOSE)
- Reinforce value
- Add urgency (limited slot / batch)
- CTA button:
  - Join Now
  - Chat WhatsApp

---

## 12. CONTACT INFO
- Email
- Phone
- Social media

---

# DESIGN DIRECTION
- Clean, modern, feminine
- Soft colors (beauty & wellness vibe)
- Clear hierarchy
- Mobile-first
- CTA visible multiple times

---

# OUTPUT FORMAT
- Full landing page copy (ready to use)
- Structured in sections
- Include headline variations (3 options)
- Include CTA variations
- Include microcopy (button text, helper text)

---

# EXTRA INSTRUCTION
- Prioritize clarity over creativity
- Make it conversion-focused
- Avoid long paragraphs
- Use bullet points where needed
- Make every section answer: “Why should I join?”

---

# FINAL GOAL
User feels:
👉 “Ini gampang dimulai”
👉 “Ini sudah ada sistemnya”
👉 “Ini peluang yang masuk akal”
👉 “Saya mau coba”
