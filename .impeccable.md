# Ginabo — Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
Indonesian women (Gen Z / young millennial, mobile-first, urban) browsing Ginabo's skincare e-commerce site to research and buy skincare online. They compare products, check BPOM/Halal certification and ingredients, and often decide during short mobile sessions (commute, break, evening scroll before checkout).

### Brand Personality
"Skincare Friendly Expert, seperti teman yang paling paham kulitmu" (a friendly expert who truly understands your skin) — Ginabo's own footer tagline. Three words: **warm, trustworthy, approachable-premium**. Not clinical/cold, not loud/gimmicky — reads like a knowledgeable friend, not a lab coat.

### Aesthetic Direction
Reference: somethinc.com's product detail page — borrowed for its **structural clarity, typography confidence, and motion polish** (smooth tab transitions, scroll-triggered reveals), *not* its color palette or copy. Ginabo keeps its own purple identity (`#78257C` family, `brand.*` Tailwind tokens) and all existing Indonesian copy/product data/images — this is a UI/motion-quality upgrade, not a content rewrite.

Anti-reference: generic templated AI e-commerce look — rounded-everything cards with no hierarchy, timid pastel gradients, zero motion intent, everything centered.

Theme: **light**. Product photography and ingredient/certification info need a light, neutral surface; consistent with the rest of the storefront (already light, using `brand.50/100` neutrals).

### Design Principles
1. Motion is purposeful, not decorative — gallery transitions, tab switches, and scroll reveals communicate a state change, not just "look fancy." No animation without a reason.
2. Preserve all existing Ginabo copy, product data, and image assets — every change here is structure/typography/motion, never content.
3. Typography carries real hierarchy — a confident display treatment for product name/price, a calmer voice for tab body copy — not one flat size scale repeated everywhere.
4. Stay inside Ginabo's existing warm-neutral surface (`brand.50/100`) — don't import Somethinc's cooler grays or their purple hex values.
5. Every interactive element (variant/qty selector, tabs, add-to-cart, gallery thumbnails) has a real motion state — press feedback, focus, transition — never an instant snap.
