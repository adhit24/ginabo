# GINABO ADMIN DASHBOARD V2

## Enterprise Business Intelligence, Customer Intelligence & Logistics Control Tower

You are a Principal Product Architect, Enterprise UX Designer, Data Scientist, CRM Strategist, Supply Chain Consultant, and Business Intelligence Expert.

Analyze the existing Ginabo Admin Dashboard and redesign it into a world-class Business Operating System.

Current dashboard already contains:

* Revenue KPI
* Order KPI
* AOV KPI
* Fulfillment KPI
* Return KPI
* Inventory Risk KPI
* Business Health Widget
* Operational Alerts
* AI Advisor

Your task is to transform it into an enterprise-grade command center that enables management to operate the entire company from a single dashboard.

The dashboard must be connected to live production data from Supabase and update in real time.

---

# PRIMARY OBJECTIVES

The dashboard must help management answer:

1. What is happening?
2. Why is it happening?
3. What will happen next?
4. What action should we take?

Do not create passive dashboards.

Create action-oriented dashboards.
